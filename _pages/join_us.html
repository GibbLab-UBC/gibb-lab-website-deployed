Join Us

We're always looking for curious, motivated, and collaborative individuals who are excited to tackle challenging questions in cancer research. The Gibb Lab brings together computational and biological perspectives, and we value trainees with diverse backgrounds, experiences, and research interests.

We welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students, postdoctoral fellows, research staff, and trainees whose interests align with the work of the Gibb Lab. Even when a formal position is not available, we encourage interested candidates to introduce themselves and tell us about their background, research experience, and future goals.

Interested in Joining the Lab?

Email us at ubcgibblab@gmail.com to introduce yourself and tell us a little about your background and research interests.

Please use the following subject line: [PROSPECTIVE POSITION] – [Your Full Name] (e.g., [PROSPECTIVE MSc STUDENT] – Jane Doe)

Please include:

  • Cover letter
  • CV
  • Position of interest and expected start date
  • Unofficial transcripts, for prospective students
  • Contact information for 2–3 references, if applicable
