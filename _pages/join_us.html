Join Us

The Gibb Lab is a computational and translational cancer research group focused on cancer genomics, transcriptomics, and digital pathology.

We are particularly interested in prospective trainees with demonstrated experience in bioinformatics, computational biology, statistics, or related quantitative disciplines. Strong programming skills, experience analyzing large-scale genomic datasets, and the ability to conduct research independently are highly valued.

Our research is computationally intensive, and successful candidates are expected to have an established technical foundation that enables them to contribute meaningfully to ongoing projects.

Due to limited training capacity, we prioritize applicants whose existing skills and research experience closely align with our current research programs.

Interested in Joining the Lab?

Email us at ubcgibblab@gmail.com to introduce yourself and tell us a little about your background and research interests.
